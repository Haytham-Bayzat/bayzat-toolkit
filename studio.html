What is in it

Five parts, and what each one takes off your plate

You do not need all of them. Most teams start with one or two and add the rest later.

SD

Service Desk

Standard

Employee requests in one place instead of scattered across chats and inboxes.

  • Every request gets an owner, a status and a response time
  • Staff can see where their own request has got to without chasing anyone
  • Categories route questions to the right person automatically
  • Nothing disappears when someone is on leave
HR stops being the bottleneck for every small question
WF

Workflows

Standard

Routine steps happen on their own, triggered by what employees actually do.

  • An event starts it, your rules decide if it runs, the platform does the rest
  • Onboarding emails, approval routing, manager notifications, handovers between teams
  • Every run is logged, so you can see exactly what fired and when
  • Pause, edit or switch any workflow off at any time
Nothing depends on someone remembering to do it
TM

Task Manager

Standard

Internal work with a named owner and a deadline, visible without chasing.

  • Assign tasks to people with due dates and track them to done
  • A dashboard showing what is assigned to you and what you assigned to others
  • Overdue items surface instead of quietly slipping
  • Big pieces of work broken into parts people can actually pick up
You stop asking "where did we get to with that"
