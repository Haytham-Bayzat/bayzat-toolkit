How to work it

1. Get the date

One line, dropped into a conversation you are already having. Framed as record keeping, not a pitch.

"While I update your account record, when does your medical cover renew and who handles it at the moment?"

2. Qualify before you work it

At around 90 days out, ask for the table of benefits and the member list, then send it to the insurance team. They decide whether the premium is worth pursuing. Do not build anything before that comes back.

"Send me your current table of benefits and member list and I will have our insurance team put a comparison together."

3. Lead with the admin

Every broker promises a better rate. Only you can say their census already sits in Bayzat, so there is no spreadsheet to build and additions and deletions happen where their team already works.

"Your census is already in Bayzat, so I can get this quoted without you building anything."
