What happens after you sign

No long implementation project. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

1
SignOrder form and MSA through PandaDoc, signed the same day if you are ready. Day 1
2
Documentation meetingYour implementation specialist meets you to understand your policies and explain what is needed. 1 to 2 days
3
Document collectionYou complete the Bayzat onboarding sheet, with your specialist on hand throughout. Your pace
4
Document reviewYour specialist checks everything for accuracy before configuration begins. 1 to 3 days
5
Account configurationYour account is built around your documents and the features you have subscribed to. 8 to 10 days
6
Feature trainingLive training sessions for you and your stakeholders on the features you have taken. 4 to 7 days
7
Employee activationYour Customer Success Manager rolls Bayzat out to the whole organisation. 2 to 3 days
Around three to four weeks from signature to go live. The one variable is how quickly the employee documents come back, everything else is on us.
