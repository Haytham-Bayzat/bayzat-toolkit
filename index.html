How to use these

  1. Open the one you need.
  2. Click Edit details in the top right and fill in your client.
  3. Click Update to see it, then Save as new file to keep a copy for that account.
  4. Use Save client copy when you want to send it to the client, which strips out the pricing controls.
  5. Print or PDF gives you something you can attach to an email.
Everything here loads with sample numbers for a made up company. Replace them with the real figures before you show anything to a client, and never upload a filled in insurance tracker to this site.
